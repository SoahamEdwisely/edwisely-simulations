One delivery, five ideas. An autonomous pizza car, RoboCar, drives from the shop to a customer’s home. On the way it shows intelligence, uses artificial intelligence, behaves as an agent, makes a rational choice and follows a logical rule.

These words are often used as if they meant the same thing. Watching them side by side in one journey shows how they differ.

Intelligence — see, understand, adapt. AI — a machine doing a job that needs a human brain, usually learned from data (reading a traffic light). Agent — something that senses and acts on its own towards a goal, in a sense → think → act loop.

Rationality — choosing the action with the best expected outcome for the performance measure (safe and on time). Logical reasoning — strict IF–THEN rules whose conclusion is guaranteed whenever the condition is true.

Reading the traffic light is AI: a vision model trained on millions of images says “red, 98% sure”. It is very good, but it is a learned guess.

Stopping for a person is logic: IF person_in_path THEN stop. Once the condition is true, the conclusion follows with certainty. Real self-driving cars combine both — learned perception feeding hard safety rules.
