Linear regression finds the straight line that best describes how one quantity y changes with another, x: ŷ = m·x + b. It is the first model most machine-learning courses teach, and still one of the most used.

Once fitted, the line predicts y for any x — including values it never saw.

The left view is the data with the fitted line. The right view plots each point’s residual e = y − ŷ, its vertical miss.

The line is found by least squares: m = Sxy ÷ Sxx and b = ȳ − m·x̄, which minimise the sum of squared residuals. R² = 1 − SSE ÷ SST says what share of y’s variation the line explains. The 95% confidence band shows where the true mean line probably lies.

  1. Tap the graph to add points; drag a point to move it, or drag it off the graph to remove it.
  2. Watch m, b and R² update live, with the working in Least squares.
  3. Drag the purple marker on the x-axis to make a prediction.
  4. Press Add outlier and see the line tilt and R² fall.
  5. Pin a run, switch data, and compare.

Keys: P pin · O outlier · Del remove the selected point · R reset · F full screen · Esc this index.

Study hours vs exam score: m = 7.91, b = 38.4, R² = 0.971. Each extra hour of study goes with about 7.9 more marks, and the line explains 97% of the variation in scores. At 5.5 hours it predicts about 81.9%.

The same fit prices houses by floor area (about $2.73k per m² here) and estimates revenue from ad spend. Regression shows association, not cause: more study and higher scores may share other reasons.

    Key ideas

    What to notice.

    • Least squares picks the one line with the smallest sum of squared residuals.
    • Slope m is the change in ŷ for one unit of x; b is ŷ at x = 0.
    • A residual e = y − ŷ is the vertical miss. Squaring makes big misses count much more.
    • Outliers far from the trend pull the line towards them and usually lower R².
    • R² is the share of the variation in y the line explains: 1 is perfect, 0 is no better than ȳ.
    • Residuals should look like random scatter about 0; a pattern means a straight line is the wrong model.
